How this works
  1. RDP presort — simplifies the raw shot chain using a tolerance of half the pipe width, so tighter pipe keeps more detail and wider pipe forgives more meander.
  2. Line fit — each candidate segment is fit with total-least-squares (2D PCA), which handles any orientation, not just non-vertical runs.
  3. Bend merge — neighboring segments are folded together, least-risky first, whenever their deflection is under the major-bend threshold or a segment has too few points to trust its bearing yet.
  4. Outlier rejection — points sitting far outside a segment's own noise level (a robust MAD-based check) are dropped and the segment is refit.
  5. Vertices — reconstructed as the geometric intersections of consecutive fitted lines, not as any single raw survey shot.
